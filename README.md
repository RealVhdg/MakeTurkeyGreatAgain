# MakeTurkeyGreatAgain 🇹🇷

**Your internet. Your privacy. Your freedom.**

MakeTurkeyGreatAgain is an open-source project focused on internet freedom, digital privacy, and online accessibility in Türkiye.

As new restrictions and verification requirements are introduced, we believe that privacy, transparency, and digital rights deserve more attention.

## Our Mission

Our goal is to research modern internet restrictions, explore privacy-focused technologies, and develop accessible tools that help users better understand and protect their digital experience.

We believe the internet should remain open, secure, and accessible.

## Project Status

**Early Development**

This project is currently in its early stages. More information, technical documentation, and updates will be available as development progresses.

## Open Source

Transparency matters. This project aims to remain open-source and community-driven.
